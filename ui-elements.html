Magnetostrictive Sensing Technology

The Villari Effect in Practice

Non-Destructive Stress Measurement

The magnetoelastic effect (also known as the Villari effect) describes the change in magnetic permeability of a ferromagnetic material when subjected to mechanical stress. This principle forms the foundation of all Zyglox sensing products.

By measuring the magnetic field response, we can determine the exact stress state of a component without physical contact or surface preparation.

  • Non-contact measurement principle
  • No shaft modification required
  • Works through air gaps up to 5mm
  1. Install sensor coil assembly around target shaft
  2. Calibrate baseline magnetic field signature
  3. Begin continuous real-time stress monitoring
The Villari effect enables truly non-invasive structural health monitoring at industrial scale.
