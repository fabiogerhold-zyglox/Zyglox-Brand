01. LOGO_SYSTEM

The primary Zyglox logomark. Use the white version on dark backgrounds and the black version on light backgrounds. Never use the logo on backgrounds that compromise legibility or contrast.

PRIMARY // ON LIGHT

// USAGE RULES

CLEAR SPACE

Always preserve minimum clear space of X (equal to the height of the "Z" character) around all sides of the logo. Do not crowd the mark with other elements.

20px MIN

MINIMUM SIZE

Never render the logo smaller than 20px in height for digital use, or 10mm for print. Below this, legibility of the logomark is compromised.

COLOR

The logo exists only in white (for dark backgrounds) and black (for light backgrounds). Never apply tints, gradients, or accent colors to the logomark itself.

CONTRAST

The background behind the logo must provide sufficient contrast (minimum 4.5:1 luminance ratio). Avoid busy photos, patterns, or mid-tone colors that reduce the mark's clarity.
